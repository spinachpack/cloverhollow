# Clover Hollow: Endings Guide (SPOILERS)

## How the last month works
At the Spring Festival (month 36) the game checks things in this order:
1. **The Hearing** decides the town's fate: *saved*, *compromise* or *lost*. This is mentioned in whichever ending you get.
2. **"The Whole Town Came"** is checked first (see below).
3. The friend with the **highest affection (70+)** confesses. Say yes for a partner ending (your career is shown too). Say no and you get a career ending.
4. Otherwise a **career ending** is picked, checking the list below from the top.

## Partner endings (affection 70+ with the friend, and say yes)
| Friend | Ending | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Gareth | Shield Beside Shield | Strength 60+ and Nerve 45+ |
| Gareth | The Captain's Steady Hand | Anything else |
| Fenn | Two Lamps in the Archive | Wit 60+ |
| Fenn | The Riddle-Thieves of Clover Hollow (secret) | Wit under 60 and Nerve 50+ |
| Fenn | Fenn's Last Riddle | Anything else |
| Mugi | The Bakery by the Fence | Craft 50+ |
| Mugi | A Very Long Fence | Anything else |
| Suzu | Suzu & (You), General Goods | Charm 50+ and 500+ coins |
| Suzu | The Rival Who Stayed | Anything else |

If two friends are at 70+, the one with the higher affection confesses.

## Secret endings
| Ending | Needs |
|---|---|
| The Whole Town Came | Affection 55+ with ALL four friends and Kindness 65+. This beats the partner endings. |
| Keeper of the Clover Charter | All four Charter pieces and the Hearing result "saved" (and no partner ending). |
| Mayor of Clover Hollow | Renown 11+, and Charm, Wit and Kindness all 55+. |

## The Hearing (the town's fate)
Score = 15 per Charter piece + your approach + Renown x2 (max 16) - 10 if you took the Steward's coins.
* **85+** saved, **50+** compromise, otherwise lost.
* Approach: Argue the law = Wit x 0.45. Speak to the heart = average of Charm and Kindness x 0.4. Stand your ground = Nerve x 0.4. River road = average of Wit and Craft x 0.4, plus 15 if you learned Brock's reasons.

## Handy details
* **Charter pieces:** after Grandma's first letter (January, Year 1), each friend gives a piece once your affection with them is 50+.
* **Learning Brock's reasons:** at the bridge scene (October, Year 3) answer "build the road, just not through the pasture" or "I'm sorry", or ask him "why" at the market in Year 2.
* **Renown:** contest podiums (gold +3, silver +2, bronze +1; there are 5 contests), a great job result +1, refusing Brock's coins +1, volunteering to repaint the bridge +1.
* **Affection:** time with friends, cameos during activities, and event choices. Their favorite stats help: Gareth (Strength, Nerve), Fenn (Wit, Charm), Mugi (Kindness, Strength), Suzu (Charm, Wit).
